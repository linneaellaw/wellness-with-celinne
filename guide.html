Celinne's Cannes

CANNES Home on the Riviera

  1. 01

    La Môme Plage

    Beach lunch · La Croisette

    Long lunches by the sea, striped parasols and Slim Aarons photographs on the walls. Pure Riviera glamour, the way I love it.

  2. 02

    Marché Forville

    Market · Le Suquet

    The covered market for fruit, flowers and fish. Go early in the morning, then have a coffee nearby while the town wakes up.

  3. 03

    Le Suquet

    Walk · Old town

    Climb the little streets of the old town up to the church for the best view over the bay. Beautiful at sunset.

  4. 04

    Îles de Lérins

    Day trip · A short boat ride

    Take the boat to Sainte-Marguerite or Saint-Honorat for pine forests, clear water and a slower rhythm. My idea of a reset day.

  5. 05

    Astoux et Brun

    Seafood · Centre

    A Cannes classic for oysters and seafood platters. Uncomplicated, lively and always good.

  6. 06

    L'Uppercut

    Boxing · Cannes

    My new favourite way to train in Cannes. Focus, rhythm and footwork, which, as a dancer, I love. Read my story.

Celinne's Nice

NICE Old town & sea

  1. 01

    Cours Saleya

    Market · Vieux Nice

    Flowers, produce and the colours of the old town. A morning stroll here is how I like to start a day in Nice.

  2. 02

    Colline du Château

    Walk · Above the old town

    Walk up for the panorama over the Baie des Anges and the port. Worth every step, especially early.

  3. 03

    Le Plongeoir

    Dinner · Bord de mer

    A restaurant perched on a rock above the sea, on the old diving board. Book a table at sunset.

  4. 04

    Chez Pipo

    Socca · Port

    The place for socca, the crispy chickpea pancake of Nice. Simple and delicious.

  5. 05

    Fenocchio

    Ice cream · Vieux Nice

    An old-town institution with flavours you will not find anywhere else. Try something unexpected.

  6. 06

    Musée Matisse

    Art · Cimiez

    Matisse in the hills of Cimiez, surrounded by olive trees. A calm, light-filled afternoon.

Celinne's Stockholm

STOCKHOLM Where it began

  1. 01

    Sturebadet

    Spa · Östermalm

    A historic bathhouse from 1885 in the middle of the city, with a pool and a Turkish bath. My favourite way to warm up in winter.

  2. 02

    Rosendals Trädgård

    Garden café · Djurgården

    Fika among the greenhouses and fruit trees on Djurgården. Take the long walk there along the water.

  3. 03

    Vete-Katten

    Fika · City

    An old-fashioned konditori for a proper Swedish fika. Order a cinnamon bun and take your time.

  4. 04

    Östermalms Saluhall

    Food hall · Östermalm

    The grand food hall for Swedish classics. Perfect for a shrimp sandwich at lunch.

  5. 05

    Fotografiska

    Photography · Södermalm

    Photography exhibitions with a beautiful view over the water. Stay for a drink after.

  6. 06

    Fjäderholmarna

    Archipelago · By boat

    The closest taste of the archipelago, just a short boat ride from the city. Summer at its most Swedish.

Celinne's Dubai

DUBAI Desert glamour

  1. 01

    Al Fahidi

    Old Dubai · Bur Dubai

    Wander the wind-tower houses and quiet lanes of the old quarter, then cross the Creek on a wooden abra.

  2. 02

    Arabian Tea House

    Breakfast · Al Fahidi

    Breakfast under the tree in a courtyard in old Dubai, with a huge choice of teas.

  3. 03

    Alserkal Avenue

    Art · Al Quoz

    Warehouses full of galleries, concept stores and good coffee. My favourite creative corner of the city.

  4. 04

    Kite Beach

    Morning move · Jumeirah

    A sunrise walk or run along the sand with the Burj Al Arab in the distance, before the heat sets in.

  5. 05

    Madinat Jumeirah

    Evening · Umm Suqeim

    Waterways, wind towers and lantern-lit terraces. Lovely for an evening stroll and dinner.

  6. 06

    The desert at dawn

    Escape · Al Marmoom

    Drive out early and watch the sun rise over the dunes. The silence is the luxury.

Celinne's Bali

BALI Slow living

  1. 01

    The Practice

    Yoga · Canggu

    An open-air bamboo shala in the rice fields of Canggu, devoted to traditional Hatha yoga.

  2. 02

    The Yoga Barn

    Yoga · Ubud

    The classic place to practise in Ubud, with classes from early morning to evening.

  3. 03

    Campuhan Ridge Walk

    Walk · Ubud

    A gentle ridge walk through tall grass and palms. Go at sunrise, before the heat.

  4. 04

    Tirta Empul

    Temple · Tampaksiring

    The holy water temple, where people come for a purification ritual. Dress respectfully and go early.

  5. 05

    Sidemen

    Rice terraces · East Bali

    The Bali of the postcards: green terraces, Mount Agung and real quiet, far from the crowds.

  6. 06

    Uluwatu Temple

    Sunset · Bukit Peninsula

    A clifftop temple above the ocean, with the Kecak fire dance at sunset. Unforgettable.

Cannes·New opening·Fitness

A knockout evening Mondrian × L'Uppercut

An evening of boxing in Cannes, and my new favourite place to train.

~

I went along to the Mondrian × L'Uppercut event in Cannes, and I left with a big smile and slightly tired arms. Boxing is such a wonderful way to move: it is focus, rhythm and footwork, which, as a dancer, I love.

If you have been curious about boxing, this is the spot I recommend for lessons in Cannes. Whether you are a complete beginner or simply want a new way to train while you are on the Riviera, it is a lovely place to start.

Celinne's tip

Book a lesson, bring water and a good playlist in your head, and let yourself enjoy it.
