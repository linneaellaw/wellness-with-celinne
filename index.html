About Celinne

Private dance lessons and massage, wherever you are on the Riviera.

Let me introduce myself. My name is Celinne Karlsson. I'm originally from Sweden, and my love for movement began on the ballroom floor, competing across Europe. Life then took me to Oslo, London, Paris, Dubai and Bali, and eventually to the French Riviera, which I now call home.

Along the way I discovered another passion: massage, and how important it is to feel at home in your own body. Health and beauty have always been close to my heart.

Today I bring the two things I love most together: Dance and Massage. Private lessons, personalised treatments and joyful gatherings on the Côte d'Azur, at a spa, in your hotel suite, at your villa or on your yacht.

A little Scandinavian simplicity, a little Riviera indulgence, and always a reason to feel good.

With love,Celinne
