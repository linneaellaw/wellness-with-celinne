About Celinne

Wellness with Celinne is an invitation to move from joy, to rest under a Scandinavian touch, and to fall a little more in love with the Riviera.

Let me introduce myself. My name is Celinne Karlsson. I'm originally from Sweden, but I have spent a large part of my life abroad. Oslo, London, Paris, Dubai, Bali and Cannes are all places where I feel at home.

When I was younger I had a career in ballroom dancing, competing around Europe. Since then I have had many different occupations: bartender, retail, make-up artist, fitness instructor, dance teacher, real estate agent, eyelash extensionist… the list is long.

Health and beauty have always been close to my heart. Today I bring together the two things I love most: dance and massage, at a spa, in your hotel suite or at your villa.
